The Prayer App · com.prayerapp1.app Privacy Policy Effective November 9, 2026 The Prayer App is designed to function offline and keep your personal prayer and study information on your device. This policy describes how the current Android app handles information. Information stored on your device The Prayer App stores information you enter or configure, such as prayer schedules, prayer requests, journal entries, fasting history, Bible bookmarks and annotations, preferences, and reminder settings locally on your device. This information is used to provide the app’s features. Location With your permission, the app accesses device location to calculate sunrise- and sunset-based prayer times. Location access is optional; related scheduling features may be limited without it. The current app does not use internet access to transmit location to the developer. Notifications and alarms If enabled, Android notifications and scheduled alarms provide prayer and other reminders. You can change notification and alarm permissions in your device settings. Backups and exports You may export app data and later import it. The app offers password-encrypted backup options. You choose where exported files are stored or shared. If you upload or share a backup using another service, that service’s privacy practices apply. Keep your backup passwords and files secure. Information received by the developer The current version is designed to operate offline and does not include an internet permission, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. It does not automatically send your prayer entries, journal content, or location to the developer. If you email us feedback or a support request, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond and improve the app. Please avoid including sensitive journal or prayer content in support messages. Data retention and deletion Locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app, subject to your device’s behavior. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them. To request deletion of support correspondence you sent us, email the address below. Intended audience The Prayer App is intended for teenagers ages 13 and older and adults. It is not designed or marketed specifically for children under 13. Security The app uses Android app-private storage and offers optional password-encrypted backups. No digital storage system is completely risk-free; protect your device and backup files. Third-party content The app includes Bible texts and educational materials with applicable attribution and source information in the app. Some explanatory educational material may have been developed with AI assistance. Changes to this policy We may update this policy when the app changes, including if we introduce payment processing, analytics, cloud synchronization, or other online features. We will update the effective date and relevant disclosures before such features are released. Contact For privacy questions or support, email theprayerapp.info@gmail.com.
